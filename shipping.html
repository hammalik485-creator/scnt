Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about getting your SCNT.

We keep it simple and fast. Order today, and your fragrance is on its way — with Cash on Delivery available across the country.

Delivery time

Orders are delivered within 2–3 days after your order is confirmed. Once you place an order on WhatsApp, we confirm your details and dispatch right away.

Delivery charges

  • FREE delivery on every order — anywhere in Saudi Arabia.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) available across Saudi Arabia — pay when your order arrives.

Returns & exchanges

If your item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, message us within 3 days of delivery and we'll replace it or make it right. For hygiene reasons, opened or used fragrances can't be returned unless faulty.

How to reach us

The fastest way to get help with an order is on WhatsApp — we usually reply within a few minutes.
