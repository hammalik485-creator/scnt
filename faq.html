Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things customers ask us most.

Are these original branded perfumes?
SCNT fragrances are inspired-by versions of the world's most iconic scents. We are not affiliated with the original brands. We recreate those scent profiles using premium fragrance oils, so you get a very similar experience at a fraction of the price.
How long does the scent last?
Thanks to our high oil concentration, most fragrances last a full day on skin and even longer on clothing. Longevity varies a little by scent and skin type.
How long does delivery take?
Orders are delivered within 2–3 days after your order is confirmed. See our Shipping & Returns page for full details.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes — Cash on Delivery is available nationwide. You pay when your order arrives at your doorstep.
How do I place an order?
Add the fragrances you love to your bag, then tap “Order via WhatsApp.” Your order details are filled in automatically — just send the message and share your name and address to confirm.
What are the delivery charges?
Delivery is FREE on orders over Rs. 3,000. A small flat charge applies below that, confirmed with you on WhatsApp.
Can I return or exchange a product?
If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, message us within 3 days of delivery and we'll replace it. For hygiene reasons, opened or used fragrances can't be returned unless faulty.
Do you sell gift bundles?
Yes! Our Gift Bundles pair popular scents together at a discounted price — perfect for gifting or building your collection.
